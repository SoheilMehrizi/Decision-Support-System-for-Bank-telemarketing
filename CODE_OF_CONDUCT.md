## Code of Conduct

We are committed to fostering a welcoming and harassment-free community for everyone.

### Our Standards
- Be respectful, considerate, and collaborative.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

### Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards. Unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue. Maintainers will review and respond as appropriate.

### Attribution
This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant (v2.1).
